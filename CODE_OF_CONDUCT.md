# Code of Conduct

Realtysharks is built for professionals, and its community should feel the same: respectful, direct and welcoming to people of every background and level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be considerate and assume good intent; critique the work, not the person.
- Give and accept feedback on code and ideas graciously.
- Keep discussion on topic and in public channels unless it involves someone's private information or a security issue.
- Respect people's privacy: never post someone else's personal data, and never post real user or customer data from a deployment.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or imagery, personal or political attacks, unwelcome sexual attention, threats, sharing someone's private information without permission, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when you represent the project elsewhere. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, [@Priiiyanxhu](https://github.com/Priiiyanxhu), using the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
